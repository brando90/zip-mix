### Overall Recommendation
To definitively answer *“does ZipMix fix DoReMi?”* the experiment must:

1. **Boost statistical power** (≥ 10 seeds or alternative aggregation).  
2. **Ensure true replication** of DoReMi’s training corpus and hyper‑parameters.  
3. **Add missing controls** (token‑proportional over buckets, explicit contamination thresholds).  
4. **Formalize reproducibility checks** (config diffs, seed logs, container versions).  
5. **Clarify decision criteria** to require both mean and worst‑domain improvements.

Addressing the critical items (1‑4) will transform the design from a high‑risk, under‑powered pilot into a robust, claim‑supporting study.
