\section{Dark-Matter Scaling}\label{sec:darkmatter}
Scaling laws fit a power curve up to $\approx10^{3}$ scale; beyond that the loss deviates unpredictably, a phenomenon termed \emph{dark matter} \citep{schaeffer2024predictive}.
All ZipMix evaluations remain below this threshold.
