\section{Discussion and Limitations}\label{sec:discussion}
Our method depends on the relevance of the chosen validation suite and on the language neutrality of compression ratio.
Multilingual corpora and radically different downstream tasks may require re-scoring.
Group-DRO adds X\,\% extra proxy compute; ZipMix-Static offers a cheaper fallback.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
% Broader Impact Statement (required for DMLR)
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\impact{%
Compel-ZipMix reweights pre-training data using a cheap, model-free compression signal aligned to a chosen validation suite.
By making mixture design transparent and inspectable (bucket assignments and per-document scores are released), the method can reduce the compute and energy cost of targeting a data mixture to a task.
A potential concern is that any validation-suite-conditioned prior can encode the biases of that suite into the resulting checkpoint; practitioners should audit the chosen suite and the induced bucket weights before deployment.
All datasets used are publicly available. }
